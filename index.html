Nice to meet you!

Hi there! I'm Happy Wang (王欢烁), a USC student majoring in Business of Cinematic Arts and minoring in User Experience. I'm a storyteller and a translator of strategy, insights, and creativity. I take things that are complicated or messy and turn them into something people can understand, and stories they can feel.

It begins with my love for film: in high school, I independently produced a 15-minute documentary selected by multiple film festivals, and I've spent over eight years as a content creator, filming vlogs, writing blogs and reviews, and exploring photography and design.

Over time, my curiosity moved backstage. I've come to love the unglamorous half of the work: building communities, planning campaigns across markets, and reading the numbers to find out what actually landed. What I like most is spotting the part that isn't working and making it better. When the process itself became the bottleneck, I built the tool that fixed it.

The same instinct draws me to themed entertainment and user experience: both come down to learning what people truly want, then shaping every detail until they feel it. Whatever I work on next, I want it to sit where creativity, data, and human insight meet.

Outside of these pursuits, you'll often find me traveling, enjoying stand-up comedy, watching musicals, exploring museums and theme parks, or experimenting with recipes in my kitchen.
