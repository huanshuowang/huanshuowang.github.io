Nice to meet you!

Hi there! I'm Happy Wang (王欢烁), a USC student majoring in Business of Cinematic Arts and minoring in User Experience. My passion lies at the intersection of storytelling, marketing, and creating immersive experiences that connect audiences around the globe.

It begins with my love for film. In high school, I independently produced a 15-minute documentary selected by multiple film festivals. Along the way, I've spent over eight years as a content creator, filming vlogs, writing blogs and reviews, and exploring photography and design.

At USC, I've grown especially drawn to understanding and optimizing user experience, which is also why I'm so fascinated by themed entertainment. There's something magical about learning what visitors truly want and turning that into memorable, lasting experiences. Looking ahead, I hope to build a career in marketing strategy, where creativity, data, and human insight come together to shape how audiences connect with brands and stories.

Outside of these pursuits, you'll often find me traveling, enjoying stand-up comedy (I'm a huge fan!), watching musicals, exploring museums and theme parks, or experimenting with Chinese recipes in my kitchen.
